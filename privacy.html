Privacy Policy

Oslo Subsea Ltd. operates a private email tool ("Gmail MCP") used solely by the account owner to read, organize and send their own Gmail messages and attachments.

The tool accesses Gmail data only for the signed-in owner and only to perform actions they request. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings.

Google user data obtained through Google APIs is used in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
